Riding a bike in Chile: an unequal possibility?

Observatory of bike usage in Chile

Image: Macarena Andrade Muñoz - 2016

"In March 2026, the first two sections (out of a total of three) of the “Nueva Ciclovía Alameda” (New Alameda Cycle Path) were inaugurated, a project that aimed to construct a total of 8 kilometres of cycle path, connecting with other cycle roads and providing more sustainable commuting for Santiago’s citizens. Nevertheless, the new Government announced the paralysis of the third part of the project, cutting the budget allocation for 2026 and 2027. The new minister said the cycle paths were “extremely expensive, and the truth is that we don’t have the priority to carry them out.” The decision sparked an intense public debate about the project’s utility and its impact on territorial equity within the city, since the missing section spanned districts with lower socioeconomic access. Through different visualisations, this project aims to identify the real possibilities for riding a bike in Chile based on socioeconomic factors, and its impacts on the environment and on reducing the vehicle fleet."
